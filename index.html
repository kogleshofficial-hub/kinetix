FIRST RUN / MOTION GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY.

Start with Easy to learn the moves, Normal to practice your timing, or Hard if you're ready for a brutal, skill-based fight. Use comfortable air gestures only, keep your space clear, and never hit a person or object.

01
ATTACK / PULSE

JAB FORWARD

Make a short, quick air jab with either hand from your ready position. Each detected jab triggers PULSE and reduces the robot's HP. Bring your hand back toward the ready position before the next jab.

TIP · Keep the wrist inside the camera frame.
02
DEFEND / GUARD

CROSS INTO AN X

Cross your forearms in front of your chest so the camera can see both wrists. Hold the X briefly to raise and maintain the shield. Uncross your arms to return to attack mode.

TIP · Don't cover both wrists behind your torso.
03
EVASION / PHASE

LEAN LEFT OR RIGHT

Lean your torso slightly to one side, then return to neutral before leaning the other way. This shifts lanes and can evade the incoming attack when timed correctly.

TIP · Small, controlled leans work best.
04
SPECIAL / BACKUP

RELEASE NOVA

Build energy during combat, then use NOVA when it is ready. The on-screen buttons always provide backup if a gesture isn't detected.

KEYS · Space punch · G/Shift guard · A/D or arrows phase · Q/Enter NOVA · R supply · E med kit
PUNCH DETECTION SENSITIVITY

HIGH is the default for deliberate, real-feeling air jabs. Use MAX only if your camera misses clear punches.

HIGH sensitivity selected — clear, purposeful jabs register while small wrist jitter is filtered out.
